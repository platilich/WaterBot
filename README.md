## README

# Water Tracking Bot

Welcome to the **Water Tracking Bot** for Telegram! This bot is designed to help you monitor your daily water intake in a simple and interactive way.

---

### 🌟 About the Bot

- **Main Functionality**: Helps users keep track of how much water they drink throughout the day.
- **Interactive Features**: Users can select different volumes of water to add via a user-friendly keyboard interface.
- **Daily Reset**: Automatically resets the water balance at midnight, giving you a fresh start every day.

---

### 🚀 Commands

- **`/start`**: Welcomes users and provides instructions on how to interact with the bot.
  
**Keyboard Options**:
  - ➕ **Add 100 ml 💧**
  - ➕ **Add 250 ml 💦**
  - ➕ **Add 300 ml 🚰**
  - 👤 **Account**: Check your current water balance.

---

### 📜 Logging

The bot tracks user actions and logs errors to ensure smooth operation and easy troubleshooting.

---

### 💼 Usage

This bot is designed for personal use, assisting you in maintaining a healthy water consumption habit. Enjoy staying hydrated! 

For any questions or feedback, feel free to reach out!
